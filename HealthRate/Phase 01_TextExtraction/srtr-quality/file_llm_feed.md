## What is SRTR?

The Scientific Registry of Transplant Recipients (SRTR) is a federally funded database that collects, analyzes, and publicly reports on the performance of all solid organ transplant programs and organ procurement organizations in theUnited States. Operating under contract with the Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) and in coordination with the Organ Procurement and Transplantation Network (OPTN), SRTR is recognized as the gold standard for transplant quality metrics due to its comprehensive, independent, and publicly accessible data.

SRTR monitors transplant quality through Program-Specific Reports (PSRs), issued twice yearly. These reports evaluate each transplant center and organ procurement organization (OPO) on key measures, including:

-  One-year patient and graft survival rates

-  Waitlist mortality rates

-  Transplant rates

-  Donor organ utilization

Using advanced statistical modeling for risk adjustment, SRTR ensures fair comparisons across centers, making it a trusted resource for patients, clinicians, payers, and policymakers.
